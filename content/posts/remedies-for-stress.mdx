In today's fast-paced world, stress and anxiety have become silent epidemics. Homeopathy offers a gentle, natural way to support the nervous system without the risk of dependency or brain fog. Here are five of the most widely used remedies:

## 1. Arsenicum Album
Best for people who feel anxious, restless, and deeply insecure about their health or safety. They are often perfectionists and might feel chilled or exhausted.

## 2. Gelsemium
Indicated for performance anxiety, stage fright, or stress accompanied by physical weakness, trembling, and mental dullness.

## 3. Ignatia Amara
The primary remedy for acute grief, emotional shock, mood swings, or disappointment.

## 4. Aconitum Napellus
Useful for sudden, intense panic attacks, often triggered by a fright, accompanied by a rapid heart rate and fear.

## 5. Argentum Nitricum
Recommended for anticipatory anxiety—apprehension before a big event, exam, or meeting, often accompanied by nervousness and digestive upset.
