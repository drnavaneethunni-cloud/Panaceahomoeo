Homeopathy is a system of alternative medicine developed in the late 18th century by Samuel Hahnemann, a German physician. It is based on two main principles:

## 1. Like Cures Like (Similia Similibus Curentur)
This principle states that a substance that causes symptoms in a healthy person can be used, in small diluted doses, to treat similar symptoms in a sick person. For example, red onions can cause watery eyes and a runny nose; therefore, *Allium cepa* (prepared from red onions) is a common homeopathic remedy for hay fever.

## 2. The Law of Minimum Dose
Unlike conventional pharmaceuticals, homeopathic remedies are prepared through a process of serial dilution and succussion (vigorous shaking). Practitioners believe this process "potentizes" the substance, transmitting its therapeutic signature while eliminating chemical toxicity.

## Safe and Personalized
Homeopathic remedies are non-addictive and have no side effects, making them exceptionally safe for newborns, pregnant women, and elderly patients.
