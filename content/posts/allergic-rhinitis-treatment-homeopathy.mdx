Allergic rhinitis is one of the most common chronic conditions affecting both children and adults. Frequent sneezing, nasal blockage, itching, and watery discharge can interfere with sleep, concentration, and overall quality of life.

Understanding Allergic Rhinitis

Allergic rhinitis occurs when the immune system overreacts to dust mites, pollen, pet dander, mold, and environmental pollutants.

* Long-Term Support: Focuses on reducing recurrent allergic episodes.

Common Symptoms

Lifestyle Tips

* Keep bedrooms dust-free.
* Wash bedding regularly.
* Stay hydrated.
* Avoid known allergy triggers.
